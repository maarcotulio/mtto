I've always wondered why some programmers have blogs. After researching and thinking about it a bit, I realized that most of them create one to organize their ideas or to learn. I'm going to follow both of these philosophies, posting technical content related to technology as well as random things I feel like sharing.

## About the technical content

I'll write about topics that interest me, which will hopefully also help someone who's studying or brushing up on a concept. I hope you find it useful.
